# Android device check

The browser tests emulate phone and tablet viewports; they do not establish how the installed app behaves on physical Android devices. Run this check once on the intended phone and once on the tablet, using [Vanessa's play site](https://example.invalid/bundled-play/) in Chrome. Record the device model, Android and Chrome versions, date and any problems observed.

1. Open the play site online and confirm its bundled files load automatically and **Play Lemmings** becomes available. Check the home screen's offline-readiness message. To check manual import separately, use the [import-only preview](https://example.invalid/import-only-preview/), choose **Add your game files**, and select an unencrypted ZIP of your own original DOS files. Each site has separate files and progress in this browser.
2. Use Chrome's **Install app** or **Add to Home screen** command and launch the installed app from its icon. Confirm that it opens in its own window and still sees the imported files.
3. Start a level and check the rescue target, tapping lemmings, selecting skills, panning and zooming in portrait and landscape. Enter fullscreen and check that the level, skills and hints remain usable; reveal a hint and its example first move, then hide it. Exit from the pause menu and check the browser's Back gesture. If native fullscreen is unavailable, confirm the expanded view and its exit control work. Pause and resume, switch apps and return, then complete a level and check that progress remains after reopening.
   In fullscreen, verify that no floating controls cover the active game. Tap the hourglass pause button immediately left of the red nuke button in the original game panel to reveal controls. Check that the opening tap does not also activate a menu item. Use zoom in and out in portrait and landscape, then check that **Reset zoom** restores the original scale and becomes unavailable until zoom changes again. Resume and check that the controls disappear; check zoom after leaving fullscreen too. Confirm the game fills the screen without cropping and the pause button and exit remain reachable.
   In **Display settings**, compare Original and Enhanced at the same moment, including after panning or zooming. Try Enhanced and the live comparison at normal and higher speeds, listen for audio glitches and check whether gameplay remains responsive. Switch back to Original afterward if preferred; graphics style is local to this device and is not in the progress backup.
4. Turn music and sound on separately and check playback after a tap, pause, resume and reopening. Preview music before starting, switch apps or lock the screen, and check it stops until you return. Also switch away immediately after **Start level**; returning should show a paused game rather than lost play time. Try the device speaker and headphones if available; check the device volume and silent mode. Sound and music start off by default.
5. While online, load the app and a level so the app shell is cached. Close it, turn on airplane mode, reopen the installed app and play the imported level. Re-enable connectivity afterward.
6. Export a progress backup and restore it on the other device after importing the game there. Confirm the last level, best score and settings appear; restoring replaces that device's progress and settings, not its game files.
7. After an update is published, reopen online and check the app still starts, retains its local data and can subsequently launch offline. If anything fails, record the exact action and visible error before clearing site data or reinstalling.

The import-only preview must never contain original game files. The owner-authorized play site is public and includes the bundled files; its separate URL is not access control. Clearing browser storage or uninstalling can remove the local files and progress; import them again if needed. Progress does not sync between devices.

On the bundled play site, verify that reopening offline works after automatic import, that **Remove game files** keeps them removed after a reload, and that manual import still works. For a separately authenticated deployment, also verify that both the site and its bundled file URLs require sign-in.
